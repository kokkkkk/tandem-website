Overview

About Tandem

Founded in September 2025, Tandem Canada is a Toronto-based social hub for Hong Kong 2SLGBTQIA+ immigrants, allies, and newcomers who appreciate Cantonese culture. We celebrate equity, diversity, and inclusion—creating a safe, affirming community where everyone belongs and builds meaningful connections.

We bring people together through three key areas:

Networking and Connection

Creating opportunities for queer Hong Kong newcomers to meet, share experiences, build friendships, and develop lasting connections.

Queer-Friendly Resources

Sharing relevant, inclusive, and affirming information, resources, and news that reflect the interests and needs of our community.

Community Engagement

Encouraging active participation, community leadership, and mutual support through initiatives, events, and volunteering.

Territory Recognition

Land Acknowledgement

Tandem Canada is located in Tkaronto, now known as Toronto, which in Mohawk means “where there are trees standing in the water”. Today, Tkaronto is covered under Treaty #13 and the Williams Treaties. It is the traditional territories of many First Peoples, including the Mississaugas of the Credit, the Anishnaabe, the Chippewa, the Haudenosaunee, and the Wendat peoples.

As we gather here today, these nations continue to experience ongoing colonization and displacement– where land acknowledgements are offered in place of land itself.

This territory is part of ‘the Dish with One Spoon’ wampum, a Treaty made between the Anishinaabe, Mississaugas, and Haudenosaunee, where nations entered into an agreement to protect the land and responsibly care for its resources in harmony together.

Historic Milestone

2026 Toronto Pride

Theme: “A Lion of Rainbow in the Dark”

In June 2026, Tandem Canada made history as the first Hong Kong community group in Canada to participate in the Toronto Pride Parade. Bringing together 100 queer Hong Kong individuals and allies, we took to the streets of downtown Toronto in a powerful celebration of pride, diversity, and community.

While Hong Kong immigrants have long been an important part of Canada's multicultural fabric, the experiences and contributions of queer Hong Kong immigrants have often remained overlooked. Through our participation in Toronto Pride, Tandem Canada brought our community into the spotlight, making our presence visible and our voices heard. We showed Canadians that queer Hong Kong immigrants are an integral part of the country's diverse social fabric — bringing with us our identities, cultures, stories, and aspirations. Our participation was more than a celebration; it was a statement of visibility, belonging, and the right to live authentically.

The theme for Tandem Canada’s participation in Toronto Pride 2026 is “A Lion of Rainbow in the Dark” — a celebration of Hong Kong identity, queer pride, and the courage to be ourselves. Lion Rock, the iconic mountain in Hong Kong, has long symbolized the indomitable spirit of the Hong Kong people: their resilience and determination. This Lion Rock spirit resonates deeply with our community, many of whom have left Hong Kong to build new lives in Canada while carrying their cultural roots, memories, and aspirations with them.

Building on the success of our participation in 2026 Toronto Pride, Tandem Canada will continue to enhance the quality, visibility, and impact of our events and programs. In 2027, we aim to strengthen our presence at Toronto Pride through expanded promotional materials, enhanced media engagement, and a dedicated vehicle to showcase our community.

Reading Circle

Monthly Book Club

In July 2026, Tandem Canada launched its monthly Book Club at the Toronto Reference Library, bringing members together to explore literature, exchange perspectives, and engage in meaningful conversations.

Held on the last Wednesday of each month, the Book Club provides a welcoming space for members to discover new voices, share their thoughts, and connect through the power of storytelling. From queer literature and sexuality to cultural identity and lived experiences, our reading selections reflect the diverse interests and experiences of our community.

Whether you're an avid reader or simply curious about exploring new perspectives, everyone is welcome to join us.

Online Community

Chat LGBT+

Chat LGBT＋ is an online safe and affirming space created specifically for the Hong Kong queer community in Canada. Moving to a new country can bring many challenges, from navigating unfamiliar environments to finding our place in a new society. Tandem believes that no one should have to face these experiences alone.

Chat LGBT＋ is more than just an online chat group. We aim to create a community where we come together to share our stories, exchange experiences, seek advice, and offer support. Whether we are navigating life in Canada, exploring our identities, or simply looking for someone who understands, we are here to listen and look out for one another.

Rooted in our shared Hong Kong identity and queer experiences, Chat LGBT＋ fosters meaningful connections and a lasting sense of belonging. It is a space where we can speak freely, feel understood, and embrace who we are — because everyone deserves a community where they can feel at home.
